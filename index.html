Welcome

This tool gives HEMMs (heavy machines) to operators, shift by shift, automatically. It picks the best combination so that production is as high as possible.

Choose your role

How it works

1. The engineer marks each machine OK, under repair or broken down, with the time it will be OK.

2. Operators mark Present or Absent and plan their leave on a calendar.

3. The tool matches the most efficient operators with the most reliable machines and shows the result on the Dashboard.

There is no password in this tool. Anyone with the link can choose any role. All data here is made up.
